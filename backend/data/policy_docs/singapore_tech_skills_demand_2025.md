# Infocomm Media Development Authority (IMDA) - Tech Skills Demand Outlook 2025
**Publishing Agency**: Infocomm Media Development Authority Singapore (IMDA)

## Strategic Workforce Insights
The Information and Communications technology (ICT) sector in Singapore continues to be a cornerstone of economic productivity. Over the 2020-2024 horizon, ICT employment grew from 118,400 to over 175,000, achieving an annualized compound growth rate (CAGR) exceeding 8%.

### High-Demand Skill Domains
- **Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning**: High surge in demand for specialized LLM fine-tuning, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and data engineering.
- **Cybersecurity & Cloud Architecture**: Multi-cloud resilience and Zero Trust architectures across government and financial services.
- **Software Engineering & Distributed Systems**: Microservices, event-driven backends, and low-latency API design.

### Compensation Trajectory
Median gross monthly income for tech roles has consistently commanded a 35% to 45% premium over national median resident wages. As of 2024, median gross salary for tech specialists is S$7,350 to S$8,800 depending on seniority and specialization.
