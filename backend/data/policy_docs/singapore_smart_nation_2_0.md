# Singapore Smart Nation 2.0 & National AI Strategy 2.0 (NAIS 2.0)
**Publishing Agency**: Ministry of Digital Development and Information (MDDI) & Smart Nation Group
**Publication Date**: 2024

## Executive Overview
Singapore launched National AI Strategy 2.0 (NAIS 2.0) with an ambitious mandate to triple Singapore's AI talent pool to 15,000 practitioners by 2026. As AI integration deepens across finance, logistics, public services, and manufacturing, the demand for tech talent is shifting from generic web development towards machine learning engineering, data architecture, high-assurance software security, and cloud optimization.

## Key Strategic Pillars
1. **Talent & Workforce Development**: Expanding the TechSkills Accelerator (TeSA) programme with enhanced training subsidies for mid-career PMETs switching into artificial intelligence and analytics.
2. **Enterprise AI Adoption**: Supporting SMEs in transitioning from legacy digitization to AI-enabled operations via IMDA's GenAI sandbox for SMEs.
3. **Infrastructure & Supercomputing**: Investing over S$500 million into National Supercomputing Centre (NSCC) infrastructure and public-private cloud partnerships.
4. **Responsible AI Governance**: World-leading AI Verify and Model AI Governance Framework for Generative AI, establishing verifiable testing regimes.

## Labour Market Implications
- Net job growth in Information & Communications has outpaced the overall economy, maintaining over 4-6% YoY expansion.
- Local resident PMET participation in the tech workforce reached 79% by end-2024, demonstrating the effectiveness of conversion programmes.
