# Ministry of Manpower (MOM) - Labour Market Report Annual Review 2024
**Publishing Agency**: Manpower Research and Statistics Department, Ministry of Manpower Singapore
**Release**: Q4 2024 / Full Year Assessment

## Key Trends in Information & Communications
1. **Employment Growth Rebound**: Following global tech sector consolidation throughout late 2022 and early 2023, Information & Communications employment resumed healthy growth in 2024, expanding by approximately 9,900 workers over the year.
2. **Retrenchments Normalized**: Quarterly retrenchments peaked at 1,420 in Q1 2023 before steadily tapering off to 420 in Q4 2024, well below historical recessionary levels.
3. **Re-entry Rate into Employment**: 6-month re-entry rates for retrenched tech workers rebounded to 78.9% by Q4 2024, with majority securing positions in financial tech, healthcare IT, and public sector modernization.
4. **Job Vacancies**: Tech job vacancies stabilized at approximately 16,500 by Q4 2024, maintaining a healthy vacancy-to-unemployed ratio of 1.75.

## Policy Recommendations
- Continued focus on mid-career conversions for tech professionals whose roles were impacted by automation or legacy tech consolidation.
- Close coordination with IMDA and WSG to align training grants with high-growth niches including Cybersecurity, AI/MLOps, and Cloud DevOps.
